Privacy Policy

HuntForge is designed to respect your privacy. We do not require an account, and our own code does not collect, store, or sell your personal information. The details below explain the limited data involved.

Location

If you tap “Use my location” in Game Lookup, the app reads your device location once and converts it on-device to a ZIP code or state so it can show the game in your area. Your location is not stored by us and is never transmitted to our servers. You can decline location access and search by ZIP or state name instead.

Advertising

HuntForge displays ads through Google AdMob to keep the app free. To do this, Google may use a device advertising identifier (IDFA) and related data to serve and measure ads. On first launch we present:

  • A consent prompt (Google’s User Messaging Platform) where required by your region’s privacy laws (e.g. GDPR/EEA, US state privacy laws); and
  • Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt, where you can allow or decline tracking. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings, and manage ad-personalization choices via “Manage privacy choices” in the app’s Settings where offered.

Google’s handling of this data is governed by Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can remove all ads by purchasing the in-app “Remove Ads” option.

Purchases

The optional “Remove Ads” purchase is processed by Apple through StoreKit. We do not see or store your payment information.

Affiliate links

Some “Shop gear” buttons open Amazon with our affiliate tag (defnota-20). If you buy through them, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Amazon’s privacy policy governs your activity on Amazon.

Children

HuntForge is intended for licensed adult hunters and is not directed to children.
