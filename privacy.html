Our apps use Google AdMob and other advertising partners to display ads. These services may collect and use information about your device and app usage to serve personalized ads.

Your Choices and Consent Management

You have control over how your data is used for advertising purposes.

Revoke or Manage Your Consent

If you previously consented to personalized ads, you can revoke or change your consent at any time by doing one of the following:

  • In the app: Go to Settings → Privacy → Manage Consent (or similar option)

Revoking consent will stop personalized advertising but may result in seeing non-personalized ads instead.
